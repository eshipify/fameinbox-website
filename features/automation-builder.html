What is WhatsApp API — and what should you know before switching?

WhatsApp API (also called WhatsApp Business Platform or Cloud API) is Meta's official way for businesses to send and receive WhatsApp messages at scale, with automation, multiple team members, and integrations — things the regular WhatsApp app can't do.

What you gain

  • Official Meta Business Partner backing
  • Multiple team members replying from one number
  • Chatbots, automation, and bulk campaigns
  • Full CRM and conversation history in one dashboard

What you should know first

  • Once a number moves to WhatsApp API, you can no longer use the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app on that same number — it becomes API-only
  • Existing chat history from the app does not carry over — conversations start fresh from the migration date onward
